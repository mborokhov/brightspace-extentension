Settings & reminders

Use the time zone shown in your course sites. Dates without an explicit zone use this setting.

West Lafayette: America/New_York. The dashboard displays deadlines in this zone.

Browser must be running. Reminders use saved deadlines, skip inferred years and items not seen for 7 days, and fire once per deadline. Keep your courses synced.

Switching school accounts? Clear this browser’s saved data first, or use a separate browser profile.
KNOW WHAT WAS CHECKED

Sync coverage

Sync revisits discovered course and assignment-list links, up to 60 pages per run. Open missing courses, Assignments, and Quizzes manually to teach it more pages. Expand or paginate lists yourself; hidden rows and syllabus PDFs aren’t collected.

Clear this workspace?

This removes saved assignments, completion marks, page history, and settings from this browser. Collection will pause until you enable it again in Settings. Your school accounts are unaffected.
