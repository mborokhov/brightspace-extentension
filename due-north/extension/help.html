↗due north

Your courses, together.

A personal assignment tracker for Purdue Brightspace and Gradescope. No API keys, backend, or paid services. This is an independent beta, not an official Purdue product.

Install in Chrome or Edge

  1. Extract the project ZIP into a folder you’ll keep on your computer.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome or edge://extensions in Edge.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the extension folder containing manifest.json.
  4. Pin Due North from your browser’s Extensions menu. Click its icon to open the dashboard.

If the browser blocks Developer mode, your organization may manage extensions. This version cannot override that restriction. Firefox and Safari are not supported.

Collect your assignments

  1. Sign in to Purdue Brightspace and Gradescope normally, including two-factor authentication.
  2. After installing, reload any tabs that were already open. Visit each course. In Brightspace, open Assignments and Quizzes. In Gradescope, open the course assignment table. Wait a few seconds.
  3. Open every page of paginated lists and expand hidden sections. Only rows loaded into the page can be collected.
  4. Return to Due North. Check Sync coverage against your actual courses and deadlines. Dates that cannot be read confidently appear in Needs review.
  5. Sync courses refreshes the home pages and discovered course/list links in temporary background tabs, up to 60 pages. Login redirects may leave a sign-in tab open. Sign in normally and run sync again.

Sync cannot guarantee that it has found every course or assignment. It does not click submission buttons, start quizzes, read answers, read syllabus PDFs, or extract announcement-only deadlines. Brightspace layouts using unsupported links or closed shadow roots may require a parser update.

Dates and completion

Choose the same time zone your school pages use in Settings & reminders. Default: America/New_York. Explicit time-zone offsets are respected. Dates without a year use the nearest year and are flagged for review; they do not trigger reminders. Dates without a time remain date-only. Unreadable deadlines are never silently replaced with midnight.

Checkboxes are personal completion marks. They do not submit work or alter either school site. Use source restores the source’s submission status. The same assignment on both platforms remains two separate entries, because the deadlines may differ. Archive an unwanted duplicate locally.

Saved assignments are not deleted just because they disappear from a scan. Archive old work yourself. Deadlines changed by an instructor update when the relevant page is read again. Last page read does not mean every course was refreshed.

Reminders and calendar export

Desktop reminders are off by default. Enable them in settings, choose a lead time, and use Test notification. Your browser must be running and operating-system notifications allowed. Due North checks about once a minute and sends one reminder per deadline; it skips completed, archived, overdue, inferred-year, and more-than-7-days-stale items. These are convenience reminders, not a guaranteed alarm service.

Export calendar downloads an .ics snapshot of all unarchived, incomplete assignments with readable dates, regardless of the current filter. Import it into your preferred calendar. It is not a live subscription; reimport behavior varies by calendar app.

Share with friends

Send the original project ZIP. Each person extracts it and follows the installation steps above. Each uses their own school login, browser storage, and settings. The ZIP does not contain anyone’s assignments or credentials. This build supports Purdue Brightspace and Gradescope; other schools’ Brightspace domains are not included.

Do not share a browser profile or private calendar exports. Use a separate browser profile per school account, or clear Due North’s local data before switching accounts. Clearing pauses collection; re-enable it in Settings after signing into the new account. The extension does not identify the signed-in person.

Privacy

Due North stores assignment titles, course names/IDs, source URLs, due dates, status, personal completion marks, and sync diagnostics in this extension’s local browser storage. It does not collect passwords, copy session cookies, upload data, load remote code, or use analytics. Sync opens the school pages you already use; those sites handle their normal sign-in and network traffic.

Permissions are limited to Purdue Brightspace, Gradescope, local storage, alarms, and desktop notifications. Pause page collection or clear the local workspace in Settings, or uninstall the extension to remove its stored data.

Development and known limits

This beta includes automated core/worker tests and browser DOM fixtures. The included fixtures are synthetic. A live Purdue and Gradescope account check is still required to validate your courses’ current page layouts. If a page yields zero rows unexpectedly, keep the original deadline as your reference and report the page type and a redacted example to the project maintainer.

For broader public distribution, the source can be submitted to Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons after live validation and store review. The ZIP is for manual installation; it is not a one-click store installer.
