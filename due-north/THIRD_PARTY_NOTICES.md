# Design credits

The Apple-style presentation follows [naplesblue/apple-design-skill](https://github.com/naplesblue/apple-design-skill), including its palette, radius/shadow tiers, and motion guidance adapted from Emil Kowalski. This is community guidance; Due North is not affiliated with Apple. No Apple fonts, SF Symbols, logos, or proprietary artwork are included.

Due North's compass and line icons are original code-native SVGs. The distributed app has no added runtime dependencies. Its PNG extension icons are exports of the SVG compass.
